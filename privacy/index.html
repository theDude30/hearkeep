Privacy policy

Hear & Keep: Sermon Notes · Effective 22 November 2026

Hear & Keep collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Where your sermons live

Recordings, notes, transcripts, summaries and your weeks are kept on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. If iCloud is on, they are also kept in your own private iCloud, so your other devices get them. Your iCloud is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer cannot read it.

Transcripts and summaries are made on your device by Apple’s own models. The recording is never sent anywhere to be transcribed.

What goes over the internet

  • Your own iCloud, to sync your sermons between your devices.
  • Apple’s speech model, downloaded once so your device can transcribe.
  • The App Store, if you buy the unlock. Apple handles the payment; the developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID.
  • A web page you choose to make (see below).

Web pages you make

When you tap Make a page for my group, the app uploads what that page shows, the sermon’s summary, main points, questions and verses, to our share service and gives you a link. Your notes, the transcript and the recording are never uploaded.

  • Anyone with the link can read the page. It is not listed or searchable.
  • It is kept until it expires (after 30 days, a year, or never, as you choose) or until you delete it in the app: Settings › More settings › Web links.
  • It is not linked to you. There is no account, and no name, email or device identifier is sent with it.
  • To limit abuse, the service counts requests per internet address per hour. The address is scrambled with the date first and is never stored.
  • Readers can report a page. A report keeps only the page, the reason chosen and the time, for 180 days. Pages that break the law or the rules can be taken down.

The share service runs on Cloudflare, which processes requests under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. The pages have no cookies, scripts or analytics.

The microphone and speech recognition

The app listens only while you are recording, and you can always see that it is. Speech recognition happens on your device. Typed notes work without the microphone.

Notifications

Daily reminders for your week are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Delete a sermon at any time; it stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, then it is gone. Delete a web page from Settings › More settings › Web links. Deleting the app removes everything on that device; to remove the iCloud copy, use Settings › your name › iCloud on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The app will never start collecting information about you.
