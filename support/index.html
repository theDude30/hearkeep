Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Your first three recorded sermons are free, with everything. After that, one purchase unlocks the app for life, for everyone in your Family Sharing group. Sermons you already have are never locked, and typed notes are always free.

I bought it before. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Hear & Keep, then go to Settings › Hear & Keep and tap Restore purchase.

My church doesn’t allow recording.

On the first screen, tap Write notes without recording. Verses still appear in full as you type them, like “Romans 8:28”.

Will it keep recording if my screen locks?

Yes. Recording carries on with the screen locked and through a phone call. If the app is closed or the phone restarts, the recording so far is recovered the next time you open it.

Why does my summary look simpler than the pictures?

The full summary uses Apple Intelligence, which only some iPhones have. On other iPhones the app makes a simpler summary from your notes and the verses. Everything else works the same.

A word in the full text is wrong.

Open the sermon, choose Full text, tap the line and choose Fix words. To teach the app a name it keeps missing, go to Settings › More settings › Names and words.

Where are my sermons kept? Are they backed up?

On your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud, so your iPad gets them too. Settings › Backups shows your backups. A deleted sermon stays in Settings › Recently deleted for 30 days.

How do I share with my small group?

Open the sermon and tap Share. Send my notes sends a tidy page. Make a page for my group makes a web page anyone can open on any phone; only the summary, points, questions and verses go on it, never your notes or the recording.

How do I delete a web page I made?

Go to Settings › More settings › Web links, and delete it there. The link stops working straight away.

Can I use my Apple Watch?

Yes. The watch is a remote: start, pause, stop and bookmark from your wrist while your iPhone records.

Report a web page

If a page made with Hear & Keep shows something it shouldn’t, use the Report this page link at the bottom of it, or email the link to tzahib@gmail.com. Pages that break the law or the rules are taken down.
